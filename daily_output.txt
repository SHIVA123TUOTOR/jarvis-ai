🎯 **Today’s Priorities:**

🛑 **Blockers / Risks:**

### **Option 2: Executive Summary** (Best for Management / Clients)

🟢 **Status:** On Track 

* **Major Win / Highlight:** [Briefly describe a key milestone or win]
