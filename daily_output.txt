Here are a few quick daily update formats depending on your needs. 

### Option 1: Standard Team/Project Standup (Best for Slack/Teams)

📅 **Daily Update — [Date]**
👤 **Name / Role:** [Your Name]

* **✅ Done Yesterday:**
  * Completed [Task 1]
  * Resolved issue with [Task/System]
* **🎯 Focus Today:**
  * Draft/build [Task 2]
  * Meeting with [Stakeholder/Team] regarding [Topic]
* **🚨 Blockers / Risks:**
  * None *(or: "Waiting on approval from [Name] for [X]")*

---

### Option 2: Executive / High-Level Briefing (Best for Management)

📊 **Daily Project Snapshot — [Project Name]**
🟢 **Status:** On Track | 🟡 At Risk | 🔴 Delayed

* **🌟 Key Win:** [Major milestone hit or major blocker cleared]
* **📌 Today’s Top Priority:** [The #1 focus for the day]
* **⚠️ Decisions Needed:** [Action item required from leadership, if any]

---

### Option 3: Fill-in-the-Blank Example (Ready to copy & edit)

📅 **Daily Update — Oct 24**

* **Completed:** Pushed code fix for login bug, finalized Q4 slide deck.
* **In Progress:** Testing checkout flow, onboarding new designer.
* **Blockers:** Need final copy from marketing to launch landing page.
