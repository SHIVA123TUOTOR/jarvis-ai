Here are a few ways to format a quick daily update, depending on who you are sending it to. 

### Option 1: Standard Project/Team Standup (Most Popular)
> **📅 Daily Update – [Date]**
> 
> **🟢 Status:** On Track 
> 
> **✅ Completed Yesterday:**
> * [Task 1 completed]
> * [Task 2 completed]
> 
> **🎯 Focus Today:**
> * [Main priority 1]
> * [Main priority 2]
> 
> **🚨 Blockers / Risks:**
> * [None / Waiting on X approval from Y team]

---

### Option 2: Executive / High-Level Summary (For Managers/Clients)
> **📊 Daily Snapshot – [Date]**
> 
> **Top Highlight:** [1 sentence summarizing the biggest win or release today]
> 
> **Key Metrics:**
> * **[Metric 1]:** [Number/Status]
> * **[Metric 2]:** [Number/Status]
> 
> **Next Milestones:**
> * [Upcoming deadline or deliverable for tomorrow/this week]
