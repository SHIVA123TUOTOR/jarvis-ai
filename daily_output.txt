Here are a few ready-to-use formats for a quick daily update, depending on what you need it for:

### Option 1: Standard Project / Team Update (Best for Slack/Teams)

📅 **Daily Update – [Date]**

✅ **Completed Today:**
* Completed [Task 1, e.g., draft of the Q3 marketing plan].
* Resolved issue with [Task 2, e.g., login bug in the staging environment].
* Held alignment call with [Person/Team].

🔄 **In Progress / Focus for Tomorrow:**
* Drafting [Task 3, e.g., project timeline].
* Reviewing feedback on [Task 4].

🚧 **Blockers / Needs Attention:**
* *None* OR *[e.g., Awaiting sign-off from Design on the new assets].*

---

### Option 2: Executive 3-Bullet Brief (Ultra-Concise)

* **Key Win:** [Main accomplishment of the day, e.g., Client approved the project proposal].
* **Current Focus:** [Top priority for tomorrow, e.g., Finalizing budget allocations].
* **Blocker/Risk:** [Any help needed, e.g., Waiting on IT for software permissions].

---

### Option 3: Personal Productivity Log

* **Top 3 Wins:** 1) ________  2) ________  3) ________
* **Unfinished Business:** ________
* **Tomorrow's Priority #1:** ________
