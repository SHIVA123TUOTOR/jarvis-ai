Here are two quick daily update templates depending on how detailed you want to be, followed by an example. 

---

### Option 1: Standard Daily Update (Best for teams/Slack/Email)

📅 **Daily Update — [Date]**

✅ **Completed Today:**
*   [Task 1 completed]
*   [Task 2 completed]

🔄 **In Progress / Focus for Tomorrow:**
*   [Task 1 in progress]
*   [Task 2 planned]

🚧 **Blockers / Needs:**
*   [None / Need approval on X from Y]

📌 **Key Takeaways / Notes:**
*   [Any quick metric, decision, or update]

---

### Option 2: Executive Summary (Best for quick reading/management)

**TL;DR:** [One sentence summarizing the biggest highlight or status of the day].

*   🏆 **Top Win:** [Major milestone or completed task]
*   🎯 **Current Focus:** [Main task being worked on]
*   ⚠️ **Needs Attention:** [Any urgent risk, decision required, or blocker]

---
